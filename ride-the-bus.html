The BEBET rule: if a card drawn matches the rank of the laid-out card, the round ends immediately. Aces are high. Between/outside compares against the first two cards. Correct guesses award 10, 20, 30, 80, or 160 B Chips by stage; a perfect run awards a 500 B Chip bonus. Your balance and best score are saved in this browser.

B Chips are arcade points with no cash value. Your balance and best score are stored locally in this browser.
